Question T2 [12 points]. Plasmonic steam generator

Focused light drives collective electron oscillations in silver nanoparticles. Some of the absorbed energy becomes heat. In water, vapour bubbles form around the particles and escape even when the bulk liquid stays below its boiling temperature.

Charge and field of a nanoparticle3.5 points

A silver sphere of radius \(R=10.0\,\mathrm{nm}\) is fixed at the origin. Each atom supplies one conduction electron. The electrons fill the sphere and the ions form a fixed positive background. Applied fields, forces and displacements are along \(x\).

2.1 0.7 points Calculate the volume \(V\) and mass \(M\) of the particle, the ion count \(N\) and the ionic charge density \(\rho\). For its free electrons find their concentration \(n\), total charge \(Q_e\) and mass \(m_0\). In subsequent parts \(Q=\lvert Q_e\rvert\).

Take the relative permittivity of every material to be one. Inside a sphere of radius \(R\) and uniform positive charge density \(\rho\), a neutral spherical region of radius \(R_1\) is centred at \(\mathbf x_d=x_d\mathbf e_x\). This is equivalent to superposing density \(-\rho\) on the original positive charge.

2.2 1.2 points Show that the neutral region has a uniform field \(\mathbf E=A(\rho/\varepsilon_0)\mathbf x_d\) and find \(A\).

2.3 1 point A uniform electron cloud of radius \(R\) and density \(-\rho\) is displaced by \(\mathbf x_p=x_p\mathbf e_x\) with \(\lvert x_p\rvert\ll R\). The volume remains neutral except at the edges. In terms of \(\mathbf x_p,n\) and the particle parameters, find the restoring force and the external work \(W_{\mathrm{el}}\).

2.4 0.6 points A uniform static field \(\mathbf E_0=-E_0\mathbf e_x\) acts on the particle in vacuum. Find the equilibrium displacement \(x_p\) in terms of \(E_0,n\) and the negative charge \(-\Delta Q\) transported across the central \(yz\) plane in terms of \(n,R,x_p\).

Equivalent circuit3.2 points

Identify charge-separation work with the energy of a capacitor holding charges \(\pm\Delta Q\). For a cloud moving at \(v\mathbf e_x\), identify its kinetic energy with the magnetic energy of an inductor carrying the current \(I\) through the central \(yz\) plane.

2.5a 0.7 points Express \(C\) using \(\varepsilon_0,R\) and evaluate it.

2.5b 0.4 points What voltage \(V_0\) on this capacitance produces \(\Delta Q\)? Express it in terms of \(E_0,R\).

2.6a 0.7 points Find \(W_{\mathrm{kin}}\) and \(I\) for a cloud moving at signed velocity \(v\).

2.6b 0.5 points Express the inductance \(L\) in terms of \(R,e,m_e,n\) and calculate it.

2.7a 0.5 points An ideal \(LC\) circuit models freely oscillating electrons after displacement. Express its angular frequency \(\omega_p\) using \(e,m_e,n,\varepsilon_0\).

2.7b 0.4 points Calculate \(\omega_p\) in rad/s and the corresponding vacuum light wavelength \(\lambda_p\) in nanometres.

Absorption and scattering4.5 points

Illuminate the particle at \(\omega=\omega_p\) with intensity \(S=\tfrac12c\varepsilon_0E_0^2=1.00\,\mathrm{MW/m^2}\). Since \(\lambda_p\gg R\), use a uniform driving field \(\mathbf E(t)=-E_0\cos(\omega_p t)\mathbf e_x\). The cloud oscillates with amplitude \(x_0\) and velocity \(v=dx_p/dt\). In a random inelastic collision an electron transfers all its kinetic energy to the ions as heat. The mean interval \(\tau=5.24\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm s\) is long compared with \(1/\omega_p\).

2.8a 1 point Find the mean Joule heating power \(P_h\) and mean square current \(\langle I^2\rangle\), explicitly retaining \(\langle v^2\rangle\) in both.

2.8b 1 point Derive the effective resistance \(R_h\) associated with heating and evaluate it.

2.9 1 point The oscillating cloud reradiates mean power \(P_s=Q^2x_0^2\omega_p^4/(12\pi\varepsilon_0 c^3)\). Derive and evaluate the analogous resistance \(R_s\).

2.10a 1.2 points Connect the equivalent elements in series with applied voltage \(V_0\cos(\omega_p t)\). At resonance derive the mean powers \(P_h,P_s\) in terms of the field amplitude \(E_0\).

2.10b 0.3 points Evaluate \(E_0\), \(P_h\) and \(P_s\).

Steam production0.8 points

A transparent vessel of dimensions \(h\times h\times a=10\times10\times1.0\,\mathrm{cm^3}\) contains \(n_p=7.3\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}\) particles. Light of the same intensity falls normally on the face of area \(h^2\). The water stays at \(20\,^\circ\mathrm C\). In steady state all Joule heat produces steam at \(110\,^\circ\mathrm C\). Vapour around the particles allows their optical response to be treated as in vacuum.

2.11a 0.6 points Find the mass of steam produced per second.

2.11b 0.2 points Calculate \(\eta=P_{\mathrm{st}}/P_{\mathrm{tot}}\), where \(P_{\mathrm{st}}\) is the power used to produce steam in the entire vessel and \(P_{\mathrm{tot}}\) is the incoming light power.
