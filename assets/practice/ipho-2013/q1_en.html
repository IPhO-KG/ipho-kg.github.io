Question T1 [9 points]. The Maribo meteorite

On 17 January 2009 a camera in Sweden recorded a fireball over the Baltic. Six weeks later a \(0.025\,\mathrm{kg}\) fragment was recovered near Maribo, Denmark. It is \(4.567\times10^9\) years old and may have come from comet Encke.

Deceleration and heating2 points

Model the body as a sphere of mass \(m=30\,\mathrm{kg}\), radius \(R=0.13\,\mathrm m\), initial temperature \(T_0=200\,\mathrm K\) and speed \(v_0=2.91\times10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}\). Treat the air density at 40 km altitude, \(\rho_a=4.1\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\), as constant. The drag magnitude is \(F=k\rho_a A v^2\) with \(k=0.60\) and projected area \(A\).

1.2a 0.7 points How long does it take to slow to \(0.90v_0\)? Keep mass and shape fixed and neglect gravity.

1.2b 0.3 points Find the ratio of the initial kinetic energy to the energy required to melt the stony meteoroid completely.

The surrounding air produces most of the glow. The homogeneous stone starts at \(200\,\mathrm K\) and its surface is held at \(1000\,\mathrm K\) during flight. Estimate the appreciably warmed depth \(x\) after time \(t\) from \(x\simeq t^\alpha\rho_{\mathrm{sm}}^\beta c_{\mathrm{sm}}^\gamma k_{\mathrm{sm}}^\delta\), with \(\rho_{\mathrm{sm}}=3300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\) and \(k_{\mathrm{sm}}=2.0\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}\).

1.3a 0.6 points Determine \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta\) by dimensional analysis.

1.3b 0.4 points For \(t=5\,\mathrm s\), find \(x\) and \(x/R\).

Dating a meteorite1.4 points

At crystallisation the minerals share a common \(^{87}\mathrm{Sr}/^{86}\mathrm{Sr}\) ratio but different \(^{87}\mathrm{Rb}/^{86}\mathrm{Sr}\) ratios. Rubidium \(^{87}_{37}\mathrm{Rb}\) decays to stable strontium \(^{87}_{38}\mathrm{Sr}\) with half-life \(T_{1/2}=4.9\times10^{10}\) years. Strontium-86 is stable.

1.4a 0.3 points Write the decay of rubidium-87 to strontium-87, including the emitted particles.

1.4b 0.7 points Show that the present \(^{87}\mathrm{Sr}/^{86}\mathrm{Sr}\) versus \(^{87}\mathrm{Rb}/^{86}\mathrm{Sr}\) data lie on a line of slope \(a(t)=e^{\lambda t}-1\), where \(t\) is time since crystallisation and \(\lambda\) is the decay constant.

1.4c 0.4 points Estimate the meteorite age using Fig. 1.2(b).

An impact on Earth2.1 points

Consider a perfectly inelastic collision with an asteroid like the one that formed the Chicxulub crater about 65 million years ago. Its radius is \(R_a=5.0\,\mathrm{km}\), density \(\rho_a=3.0\times10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\) and impact speed \(v_a=2.5\times10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}\). Earth’s moment of inertia is \(I=0.83(2/5)M_\oplus R_\oplus^2\). Neglect changes in its orbit.

1.6a 0.7 points Find the largest change in the direction of Earth’s rotation axis for an impact at the North Pole.

1.6b 0.7 points Find the change in the rotation period after a radial impact at the equator.

1.6c 0.7 points Find the change for a tangential impact at the equator in the equatorial plane.
