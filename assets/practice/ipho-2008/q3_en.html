Question T3 [10 points]. Air temperature, atmospheric stability and pollution

Vertical motions influence clouds, rainfall and the dispersal of atmospheric pollutants. A stable atmosphere limits vertical mixing. On a November morning in Hanoi a radiosonde recorded a temperature inversion above 119 m. Use these measurements to estimate the depth of the mixing layer and the concentration of carbon monoxide from morning motorbike traffic.

Treat air as an ideal diatomic gas of molar mass \(\mu=29\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}\). Use \(R=8.31\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}\), \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) and ground pressure \(p_0=101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}\). The molar heat capacities are \(c_p=7R/2\) and \(c_V=5R/2\). A quasistatic adiabatic change obeys \(pV^\gamma=\mathrm{const}\) with \(\gamma=c_p/c_V\).

Mathematical hints. An antiderivative of \(1/(A+Bx)\) is \(B^{-1}\ln\lvert A+Bx\rvert\) for \(B\ne0\). For constants \(a\ne0\) and \(b\), the solution of \(\dot x+ax=b\) can be written \(x=x_1+b/a\), where \(\dot x_1+ax_1=0\). Also \(\lim_{x\to\infty}(1+1/x)^x=e\).

An adiabatically moving air parcel3.25 points

Consider a parcel a few metres across. Its internal temperature \(T_p\) is uniform, and heat exchange with the surroundings is negligible. As it moves, its pressure stays equal to the atmospheric pressure \(p(z)\) at its centre. The ambient temperature \(T(z)\) can differ from \(T_p(z)\). In 2.1 and 2.2 no ambient temperature profile is prescribed.

2.1 1 point The parcel temperature obeys \(dT_p/dz=-G\). Find \(G\) as a function of \(T\) and \(T_p\).

Consider a profile with \(T_p(z)=T(z)\) at every altitude. The corresponding value of \(G\) is the dry adiabatic lapse rate \(\Gamma\).

2.2.1 0.5 points Derive an expression for \(\Gamma\).

2.2.2 0.25 points Calculate \(\Gamma\).

2.2.3 0.25 points How does the atmospheric temperature vary with altitude in this case?

2.3 1 point Let \(T(z)=T_0-\Lambda z\) for constant \(\Lambda\). Find \(T_p(z)\) given \(T_p(0)=T_{p0}\).

2.4 0.25 points Give an approximation to \(T_p(z)\) for \(\lvert\Lambda z\rvert\ll T_0\) and \(T_{p0}\simeq T_0\).

Morning traffic pollution in Hanoi1.25 points

Approximate the city by a rectangle of length \(L=15\,\mathrm{km}\) along the southwest bank of the Red River and width \(W=8\,\mathrm{km}\). From 7 to 8 am, \(8\times10^5\) motorcycles each travel an average of \(5\,\mathrm{km}\) and emit \(12\,\mathrm g\) of CO per kilometre. Treat the total mass emission rate \(M\) as constant during that hour.

Clean northeast wind of speed \(u=1\,\mathrm{m/s}\) crosses the sides of length \(L\) and retains its speed over the city. Mixing is fast enough to make the concentration \(C(t)\) uniform throughout \(LWH\). No pollutants escape through sides parallel to the wind. The CO concentration at 7 am is negligible.

5.1 0.5 points Derive the differential equation for \(C(t)\).

5.2 0.5 points Solve for \(C(t)\) during the rush hour.

5.3 0.25 points Calculate the CO concentration at 8 am.
