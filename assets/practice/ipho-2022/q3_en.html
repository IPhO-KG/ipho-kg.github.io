Question T3 [8 points]. Scaling laws

In the following four independent problems, determine how a measurable quantity changes when the scale of the system changes.

T3-A [2 points]. Spaghetto

T3-A1 [2 points]. A dry spaghetti strand is held horizontally at its midpoint. A strand of diameter \(d=1\,\mathrm{mm}\) breaks under its own weight at length \(\ell=50\,\mathrm{cm}\). Find the limiting length \(\ell'\) for a strand of diameter \(d'=1\,\mathrm{cm}\).

T3-B [2 points]. Sand castle

T3-B1 [2 points]. The mean volume of a coarse sand grain is ten times that of a fine grain. Form equal-sized cylinders of each sand, adjusting moisture separately for maximum strength. Compressed between parallel plates, the coarse cylinder fails at \(F_c=10\,\mathrm N\). What force breaks the fine cylinder? Neglect gravity.

T3-C [2 points]. Interstellar travel

T3-C1 [2 points]. A spaceship maintains a proper acceleration of magnitude \(g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\), as measured in its instantaneous rest frame. The entire outward and return trip must take at most 50 years on board. If \(d\) is its maximum distance from Earth, by what factor does this distance increase with proper acceleration \(g'=15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\)?

Hints. Relativistic velocity addition may be useful. Recall \(\cosh x=(e^x+e^{-x})/2\), \(\sinh x=(e^x-e^{-x})/2\) and \(\tanh x=\sinh x/\cosh x\). Useful primitives are

\[\int\frac{dx}{1-x^2}=\operatorname{artanh}x+C,\qquad \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}=\operatorname{arsinh}x+C,\qquad \int\sinh x\,dx=\cosh x+C.\]

T3-D [2 points]. That sinking feeling

T3-D1 [2 points]. A solid wooden sphere of radius \(r_0\) floats in water. Without viscous drag its small vertical oscillations would have angular frequency \(\omega_0\). With drag the measured frequency is \(0.99\omega_0\). For the same wood, find the limiting radius \(r_{\min}\) below which a small vertical displacement no longer produces oscillations.

Hint. Viscous drag is proportional to the relative speed and to the viscosity \(\eta\), whose units are \(\mathrm{kg/(m\cdot s)}\).
