Question T1 [10 points]. Permanent magnets

The NdFeB magnets have a uniform magnetization \(J=1.5\,\mathrm{T}/\mu_0\). Their hysteresis loop is broad enough for \(J\) to be treated as fixed. Here \(\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N/A^2}\) and magnetization means dipole moment per unit volume.

Hints. The series \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-4}\) equals \(\pi^4/90\). Outside a uniformly magnetized sphere the field is exactly dipolar. For a body of another shape the dipole description applies at large distances. Electric and magnetic point dipoles have the same spatial field pattern up to a factor. A boundary can sometimes be represented by fictitious sources outside the region of interest.

T1-A [4.5 points]. Interaction of magnets

Resolve a dipole moment into components parallel and perpendicular to the vector from the dipole to the observation point. Its field is

\[\mathbf B(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi r^3}(2\mathbf m_\parallel-\mathbf m_\perp).\]

T1-A1 [0.6 points]. Two coaxial cylindrical magnets have diameter \(d=20\,\mathrm{mm}\) and thickness \(h=2\,\mathrm{mm}\). They are magnetized along their common axis. Find the magnitude of their interaction force when their centres are \(L=20\,\mathrm{cm}\) apart. Use \(L\gg d,h\).

T1-A2 [0.4 points]. At distances large compared with \(h/2\), one of the magnets in A1 can be replaced by a circular current loop. Find its current \(I\).

T1-A3 [1 point]. Find the force between the same magnets when the separation of their centres is \(L=5\,\mathrm{mm}\). Work in the limit \(d\gg L\gg h\).

T1-A4 [1 point]. Identical magnetic spheres of diameter \(\delta=5\,\mathrm{mm}\) attach to one another in a chain. Holding just the top sphere, what is the greatest length \(\ell\) that can hang without breaking under its own weight? The density is \(\rho=7500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\).

T1-A5 [1.5 points]. Point \(P\) is a distance \(r\) from one end \(O\) of the chain. The line \(OP\) makes an angle \(\theta\) with the chain. Find the field magnitude at \(P\) for \(\ell\gg r\) and \(r\sin\theta\gg\delta\).

T1-B [3.5 points]. Interaction with ferromagnets

Add plates of a ferromagnetic material with constant relative permeability \(\mu_r\sim10^5\).

Hint. In the air near this material, magnetic field lines meet the surface almost normally. The electric field near a conductor behaves similarly.

T1-B1 [1 point]. Place a sphere from A4 at a distance \(s=\delta\) from a thick, infinite plate as drawn. Its magnetization is normal to the plate. Sketch the field lines through points 1, 2 and 3, following each until it closes or leaves the drawing.

T1-B2 [1 point]. Bring the sphere into contact with the plate. Indicate every stable magnetization direction in the diagram and find the normal contact force. Incorrect directions attract a deduction on the official answer sheet.

T1-B3 [1.5 points]. Sandwich the disk magnet of A1 between coaxial, thick ferromagnetic plates, each of diameter \(D=2d\). The flat faces are in contact. Find the force on each plate, neglecting the field outside the plates and the narrow gap between them.

T1-C [2 points]. Magnetic ordering

The spheres from A4 now form a large planar lattice. All moments lie in its plane and only nearest neighbours interact. In ferromagnetic order moments align. In antiferromagnetic order nearby moments oppose one another so their net field can cancel.
