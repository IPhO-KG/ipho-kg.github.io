Question T3 [10 points]. Water and Objects

Surface tension drives a drop off a surface and deforms water near solid boundaries. If needed, use \(y=Ae^{\sqrt a\,x}+Be^{-\sqrt a\,x}\) for the general solution of \(y''=ay\) with \(a>0\).

T3-A [2 points]. Coalescing drops

Two identical spherical drops rest on a strongly water-repellent surface. On contact they merge, and the new spherical drop jumps off the surface.

T3-A1 [2 points]. Find the take-off speed to two significant figures. The initial radius of each drop is \(a=100\,\mu\mathrm m\). Use \(\rho=1.00\times10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\) and \(\gamma=7.27\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{J/m^2}\). The total volume is conserved and the fraction of released surface energy converted into translational kinetic energy is \(k=0.06\).

T3-B [4.5 points]. Meniscus at a vertical plate

A thin plate lies in the \(yz\) plane. Water extends into \(x>0\) and is level at \(z=0\) far away. The profile \(z(x)\) is independent of \(y\). Measure tangent angle \(\theta\) anticlockwise from the positive \(x\) axis, with the material fixing \(\theta(0)=\theta_0\). Take density \(\rho\), surface tension \(\gamma\), gravity \(g\) and atmospheric pressure \(P_0\) as uniform.

T3-B1 [0.6 points]. For the wetting plate the liquid rises above \(z=0\). Find the water pressure at height \(z\), given \(P(0)=P_0\).
T3-B2 [0.8 points]. Isolate a slice whose free surface is at \(z_1\) on the left and \(z_2\) on the right. Find the net horizontal pressure force per unit length in \(y\). Uniform atmospheric pressure makes no net contribution.
T3-B3 [0.8 points]. The surface makes angles \(\theta_1,\theta_2\) at the left and right ends. Express the pressure force \(f_x\) through these angles and \(\gamma\) using horizontal equilibrium.
T3-B4 [0.8 points]. For either kind of plate, \(\tfrac12(z/\ell)^a+\cos\theta\) is constant on a connected surface. Find the exponent and the length scale.
T3-B5 [1.5 points]. For \(|z'|\ll1\), expand \(\cos\theta\) through second order in the slope. Differentiate the relation from B4 and solve for \(z(x)\) on \(x\ge0\) in terms of \(\tan\theta_0\) and \(\ell\).

T3-C [3.5 points]. Interaction of floating rods

Two identical rods float parallel to \(y\) at equal distances on opposite sides of the axis. Rod B is on the right. On B, the inner and outer contact lines have heights \(z_a,z_b\) and tangent angles \(\theta_a,\theta_b\), respectively.

T3-C1 [1 point]. Find the horizontal force per unit length acting on B using \(\theta_a,\theta_b,z_a,z_b,\rho,g,\gamma\).
T3-C2 [1.5 points]. Let \(z_0\) be the water height midway between the rods. Eliminate both contact angles and both contact heights from the expression for \(F_x\).
T3-C3 [1 point]. Use the small-slope equation from B5 to express \(z_0\) in terms of \(\ell\) and the inner contact point \((x_a,z_a)\) of rod B.
