Question T1 [9 pts]. Three independent problems

T1-A [3 pts]. A puck inside a rolling cylinder

T1-A1 [3 pts]. A thin hollow cylinder of mass \(M\) and radius \(R\) rests on a horizontal table. A small puck of mass \(m\) is released from rest against its inner surface at height \(R\). The puck slides without friction and the cylinder rolls on the table without slipping. Find their contact force when the puck reaches the bottom. The gravitational acceleration is \(g\).

T1-B [3 pts]. Gas inside a soap bubble

A bubble of radius \(r=5.00\,\mathrm{cm}\) in vacuum contains an ideal diatomic gas. Its film has thickness \(h=10.0\,\mathrm{\mu m}\), density \(\rho=1.10\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}\) and surface tension \(\sigma=4.00\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N/m}\). One spherical liquid–gas interface produces a Laplace pressure jump \(2\sigma/r\).

T1-B1 [3 pts]. 1) Derive and evaluate the molar heat capacity for heating so slow that the bubble remains in mechanical equilibrium. 2) Derive and evaluate the angular frequency of small radial oscillations. The film heat capacity greatly exceeds the heat capacity of the gas, and thermal equilibrium is restored much faster than an oscillation period.

T1-C [3 pts]. Current through the switch

T1-C1 [3 pts]. Initially the switch is open, capacitor \(2C\) has charge \(q_0\), capacitor \(C\) is uncharged, and the currents in \(L\) and \(2L\) are zero. Discharge begins. The switch closes instantaneously when the coil current first reaches its maximum. Find the largest magnitude of the subsequent switch current.
