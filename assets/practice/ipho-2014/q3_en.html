Question T3 [10 pts]. Simplest model of gas discharge

T3-A [4.8 pts]. Non-self-sustained gas discharge

An external ionizer produces free electrons and singly charged positive ions uniformly at a rate \(Z_{\rm ext}\) pairs per unit volume and time. An electron and an ion can recombine to form a neutral atom. The recombination rate per unit volume is \(Z_{\rm rec}=rn_en_i\), where \(r\) is constant and \(n_e,n_i\) are the two number densities.

T3-A1 [1.8 pts]. At \(t=0\) both densities vanish and the ionizer is switched on. The electron density has the form \(n_e(t)=n_0+a\tanh(bt)\). Determine \(n_0,a,b\) in terms of \(Z_{\rm ext},r\), taking \(a,b>0\).
T3-A2 [0.6 pts]. The separate ionizers produce equilibrium densities \(n_{e1}=12\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}\) and \(n_{e2}=16\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}\). Find the equilibrium density with both operating.

From here on the system is stationary. Ignore the field due to the charges themselves. The gas lies between parallel electrodes of area \(S\), separated by \(L\ll\sqrt S\) at voltage \(U\). Approximate each species density as uniform along the tube. Ions and electrons have the same drift speed \(v=\beta E\), where \(\beta\) is the mobility.

T3-A3 [1.7 pts]. Find \(I\) in terms of \(U,\beta,L,S,Z_{\rm ext},r\) and elementary charge \(e\).
T3-A4 [0.7 pts]. Find the gas resistivity \(\rho_{\rm gas}\) for sufficiently small applied voltages.

T3-B [5.2 pts]. Self-sustained gas discharge

The external source still creates \(Z_{\rm ext}\) pairs per unit volume and time. In this part the field is uniform and recombination negligible. Coordinate \(x\) runs from cathode (\(x=0\)) to anode (\(x=L\)). Let \(I_e,I_i\) denote the positive contributions of electrons and ions to the current, so \(I=I_e+I_i\).

Ions striking the cathode can release electrons. The secondary emission coefficient \(\gamma\) is the number of emitted electrons per incident ion. Electrons also ionize atoms by impact. For \(N_e\) electrons travelling through \(\mathrm dl\), avalanche multiplication obeys \(\mathrm dN_e/\mathrm dl=\alpha N_e\), where \(\alpha\) is the Townsend coefficient.

T3-B1 [2 pts]. The electron current has the form \(I_e(x)=C_1e^{A_1x}+A_2\). Determine \(A_1,A_2\) from the discharge parameters.
T3-B2 [0.6 pts]. Write \(I_i(x)=C_2+B_1e^{B_2x}\). Find \(B_1,B_2\), allowing \(C_1\) to appear.
T3-B3 [0.3 pts]. Give the boundary condition for \(I_i\) at \(x=L\).
T3-B4 [0.6 pts]. Relate \(I_i\) to \(I_e\) at \(x=0\).
T3-B5 [1.2 pts]. Express the finite stationary current in terms of \(Z_{\rm ext},\alpha,\gamma,e,L,S\).
T3-B6 [0.5 pts]. At fixed \(\alpha\), determine the critical tube length \(L_{\rm cr}\) beyond which discharge persists when the external source is switched off.
