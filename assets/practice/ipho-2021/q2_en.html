Question T2 [10 pts]. Electrostatic lens

A conducting ring of major radius \(R\) is a hollow torus with circular cross-section of diameter \(2a\ll R\) and total charge \(q\). Its axis is \(z\) and its plane is \(xy\). Use the wire thickness only in D. If needed, expand \((1+u)^s\) to second order with quadratic coefficient \(s(s-1)/2\).

T2-A [1 pts]. Field on the axis

Use zero potential at infinity. The electron has mass \(m\) and charge \(-e\) with \(e>0\). Ignore the electron's effect on the ring charge distribution.

T2-A1 [0.3 pts]. Determine the potential at axial coordinate \(z\).
T2-A2 [0.4 pts]. Keep the constant and the first position-dependent term for \(|z|\ll R\).
T2-A3 [0.2 pts]. Determine the force on an electron at A near the centre and the sign of \(q\) needed for stable axial oscillations.
T2-A4 [0.1 pts]. Determine the angular frequency of small axial oscillations.

T2-B [1.7 pts]. Field in the ring plane

At a small radial distance \(r\ll R\) in the plane \(z=0\) write \(\Phi(r)\simeq q(\alpha+\beta r^2)\).

T2-B1 [1.5 pts]. Determine \(\beta\).
T2-B2 [0.2 pts]. Find the force on the electron at B and the sign of \(q\) required for in-plane oscillations.

T2-C [2.3 pts]. Instantaneously switched lens

A nonrelativistic packet travels along the axis at speed \(v\) with energy \(E=mv^2/2\) per electron. The ring is switched immediately to \(q\) while the packet crosses \(|z|<d/2\), where \(d\ll R\), and is neutral otherwise. Take \(v_z\simeq v\) as constant and neglect field propagation delay, magnetic forces and back action.

T2-C1 [1.3 pts]. For an incoming parallel near-axis beam, find the focal length \(f\gg d\) in terms of \(\beta\) and the lens parameters. Use the focusing polarity.
T2-C2 [0.8 pts]. An on-axis point source a distance \(b>f\) before the ring emits at angles \(\gamma\ll1\). Find the image distance \(c\) after the ring.
T2-C3 [0.2 pts]. Evaluate \(1/b+1/c\) and check the thin-lens equation.

T2-D [3 pts]. Capacitance and charging

Now retain the cross-sectional radius \(a\). Useful antiderivatives are \(\int du/\sin u=\ln|\tan(u/2)|+\mathrm{const}\) and \(\int du/\sqrt{1+u^2}=\operatorname{arsinh}u+\mathrm{const}\).

T2-D1 [2 pts]. Calculate the self-capacitance of the thin conducting ring for \(a\ll R\).

On entry the ring is connected to a voltage \(V_0\), and on exit it is grounded through the same contact resistance \(R_0\). Its own resistance is negligible and its initial charge is zero. Choose the focusing polarity.

T2-D2 [1 pts]. Find and sketch \(q(t)\) and give its signed value \(q_0\) when its magnitude peaks. Time zero is passage through the ring plane; use a given \(C\).

T2-E [2 pts]. Finite switching time

Return to a parallel beam and a thin ring, retaining the charge pulse from D. The times \(R_0C\) and \(d/v\) are comparable and both short relative to \(f/v\).

T2-E1 [1.7 pts]. Find the focal length including both charging and discharging, in terms of \(\beta\) and the given parameters.
T2-E2 [0.3 pts]. What constant \(q_{\mathrm{eff}}\) replaces \(q\) in the result of C1?
