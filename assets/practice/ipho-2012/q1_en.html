Problem T1 [13 points]. Focus on sketches

T1-A [4.5 points]. Ballistics

A ball is thrown at speed \(v_0\) in a vertical plane. Take \(x\) horizontal and \(z\) upwards. Gravity has constant magnitude \(g\). Neglect air resistance.

T1-A-i [0.8 points]. For a launch from the origin, varying the angle at fixed speed gives the accessible region \(z\le z_0-kx^2\). Find \(z_0\) and \(k\). You may assume the stated shape of the boundary.

T1-A-ii [1.2 points]. The launch position is now free anywhere on the ground (\(z=0\)). Sketch the least-speed trajectory that reaches the top of the spherical building of radius \(R\). The ball may not bounce off the building first. Only the sketch receives marks.

T1-A-iii [2.5 points]. Determine the minimum launch speed \(v_{\min}\) for this trajectory.

T1-B [4 points]. Airflow around a wing

The streamlines are drawn in the wing frame. The flow is steady and two-dimensional, with no wind and a pattern independent of aircraft speed. Dynamic pressure \(\rho v^2/2\) is small compared with atmospheric pressure \(p_0=1.0\times10^5\) Pa. You may measure distances on the diagram.

At a speed well below that of sound, Bernoulli's relation may be used along a streamline. Here \(p\) is static pressure, \(\rho\) the density, \(h\) the elevation and \(v\) the flow speed.

\[p+\rho gh+\tfrac12\rho v^2=\mathrm{const}.\]

T1-B-i [0.8 points]. If the aircraft flies at \(v_0=100\) m/s relative to the ground, what is the ground speed of the air at \(P\)?

T1-B-ii [1.2 points]. At high humidity, droplets trail the wing once the aircraft exceeds a speed \(v_{\rm crit}\). Mark the point \(Q\) on the diagram where droplets first form and justify your choice using equations with minimal text.

T1-B-iii [2 points]. Estimate \(v_{\rm crit}\). The undisturbed air has temperature \(T_a=293\) K and relative humidity \(r=90\%\). Its specific heats are \(c_p=1.00\times10^3\) and \(c_V=0.717\times10^3\) J/(kg·K). Saturated water-vapour pressure is \(p_{sa}=2.31\) kPa at \(T_a=293\) K and \(p_{sb}=2.46\) kPa at \(T_b=294\) K. Relative humidity is the ratio of actual vapour pressure to saturation pressure at the same temperature. At saturation the vapour is in equilibrium with liquid water.

T1-C [4.5 points]. Magnetic straws

A superconducting cylindrical tube has length \(l\gg r\) and inner radius \(r\). Its centre is at the origin and its axis is the \(z\) axis. Flux \(\Phi\) threads the bore in the plane \(z=0\). The field vanishes within the superconducting material.

T1-C-i [0.8 points]. On an axial section draw five magnetic field lines through the five marked points.

T1-C-ii [1.2 points]. Find the axial tension \(T\) at the centre, the force between the two halves at \(z<0\) and \(z>0\).

T1-C-iii [2.5 points]. Place an identical parallel tube with its centre at \(x=z=0\), \(y=l\). Its flux has the same magnitude and the opposite direction. The tubes lie on opposite sides of a square. Find their mutual magnetic force \(F\).
