Problem T2 [8 points]. Kelvin water dropper

The water–air interface has energy \(U=\sigma S\), where \(S\) is its area and \(\sigma\) the surface tension. Adjacent surface patches exert a force of magnitude \(\sigma b\) across a common straight edge of length \(b\).

T2-A [4 points]. A single pipe

Water drips slowly from the downward-facing nozzle of a long vertical metal pipe. The nozzle's inner diameter \(d\) is much smaller than the radius \(r\) of the growing drop. Water is conducting, with density \(\rho\). The gravitational acceleration is \(g\).

T2-A-i [1.2 points]. Find the radius \(r_{\max}\) just before gravity detaches the drop.

T2-A-ii [1.2 points]. The pipe has potential \(\varphi\) relative to the distant surroundings. Find the charge \(Q\) on a drop of radius \(r\).

T2-A-iii [1.6 points]. Hold \(r\) fixed and slowly raise \(\varphi\). The drop becomes unstable and splits when its internal pressure falls below atmospheric pressure. Find the threshold \(\varphi_{\max}\).

T2-B [4 points]. Two pipes

Two such pipes share a T-junction connected to a grounded water supply. Each nozzle is centred in a cylindrical electrode of height \(L\) and diameter \(D\), with \(L\gg D\gg r\). Each nozzle releases \(n\) drops per unit time. Drops fall through height \(H\) into conducting bowls. Each bowl is wired to the opposite electrode, and the electrodes are connected across capacitance \(C\). The bowls and electrodes have zero net charge. A microscopic charge on the first drop seeds a charge imbalance.

T2-B-i [1.2 points]. When a drop detaches, one plate of the capacitor carries charge of magnitude \(q\). Express the magnitude \(Q_0\) of the drop charge using \(q\) and \(r_{\max}\) from A-i. For now ignore the instability from A-iii.

T2-B-ii [1.5 points]. Treat charge as a continuous function and find \(q(t)\) for \(q(0)=q_0\).

T2-B-iii [1.3 points]. Apart from drop breakup, electrostatic repulsion between a drop and its bowl also limits the voltage. Determine the maximum voltage \(U_{\max}\) between the electrodes set by this mechanism.
