Question T2 [10 pts]. Doppler laser cooling and optical molasses

The bright spot is a cloud of sodium atoms where three pairs of opposing laser beams intersect. At a suitable frequency the light slows the atoms. The force resembles viscous drag, hence the name “optical molasses”. Explore this mechanism in one dimension.

1 0.6 pts Photon absorption

A neutral atom of mass \(m\) travels along \(+x\) at speed \(v\). Its two internal states have energies \(0\) and \(\hbar\omega_0\), where \(\hbar=h/(2\pi)\). Initially it is in the ground state. An oncoming laser photon has lab energy \(\hbar\omega_L\) and momentum component \(-\hbar q\), where \(q=\omega_L/c\). Its frequency is resonant in the atomic frame. After absorption the atom emits along either direction of the axis with equal probability.

Assume \(v/c\ll1\) and \(\hbar q/(mv)\ll1\). Keep corrections linear in either parameter. Give signed momentum components along \(x\), and report energies and momenta in the lab frame.

1a 0.2 pts State the resonance condition for absorption.
1b 0.2 pts Find the atom's momentum after absorption.
1c 0.2 pts Find the atom's total energy after absorption.

2 0.8 pts Emission along −x

Suppose that after absorption the photon is emitted along \(-x\).

2a 0.2 pts Find the emitted photon energy.
2b 0.2 pts Find the emitted photon momentum.
2c 0.2 pts What is the atomic momentum after emission?
2d 0.2 pts What is the total atomic energy after emission?

3 0.8 pts Emission along +x

Now the photon is emitted forward, along the atom's motion.

3a 0.2 pts Find the energy of the emitted photon.
3b 0.2 pts Find the photon momentum.
3c 0.2 pts Find the final atomic momentum.
3d 0.2 pts Find the total atomic energy.

4 0.8 pts Averages after emission

Average over the two equally likely emission directions.

4a 0.2 pts Find the mean emitted photon energy.
4b 0.2 pts Find the mean emitted photon momentum.
4c 0.2 pts Find the mean total atomic energy.
4d 0.2 pts Find the mean atomic momentum.

5 0.4 pts Energy and momentum transfer

Consider one complete absorption–emission cycle.

5a 0.2 pts Find the mean change of atomic energy per cycle.
5b 0.2 pts Find the atom's mean momentum change per cycle.

6 0.6 pts Co-propagating laser beam

A laser of lab frequency \(\omega'_L\) points along \(+x\) and is resonant in the atom's frame. The atom still moves along \(+x\).

6a 0.3 pts Find the mean change of atomic energy per cycle.
6b 0.3 pts Find the mean atomic momentum change per cycle.

7 1.5 pts Force from two beams

The finite excited-state lifetime \(1/\Gamma\) broadens the resonance. For a beam of frequency \(\omega\) incident on stationary atoms, the excited fraction \(f(\omega)\) and event rate \(W\) are

\[f(\omega)=\frac{\Omega_R^2}{(\omega_0-\omega)^2+\Gamma^2/4+2\Omega_R^2},\qquad W=N\Gamma f(\omega).\]

The quantity \(\Omega_R^2\) is proportional to intensity. Now \(N\) atoms travel along \(+x\) at speed \(v\). They meet two opposing beams of equal intensity and the same arbitrary lab frequency \(\omega_L\).

7a 1.5 pts Find the force on the entire atomic beam for \(mv\gg\hbar q\).

8 2.5 pts Low-velocity limit

Take the speed sufficiently low to expand the force to first order in \(v\).

8a 1.5 pts Obtain the force in this limit.
8b 0.25 pts Which frequency gives a positive force for \(v>0\)?
8c 0.25 pts When does the force vanish for \(v>0\)?
8d 0.25 pts When is the force negative for \(v>0\)?
8e 0.25 pts Now the atoms travel along \(-x\). What frequency slows them?

9 2 pts Optical molasses

Tune the lasers for damping. At \(t=0\) the atoms have velocity \(v_0\).

9a 1.5 pts In the low-velocity limit find the speed after the lasers have been on for time \(\tau\).
9b 0.5 pts Initially the gas was in thermal equilibrium at temperature \(T_0\). Find the temperature after time \(\tau\) in this model.
