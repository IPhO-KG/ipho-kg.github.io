Problem T2 [10 points]. An accelerometer for an airbag

Consider a model vehicle accelerometer in which motion of a conducting plate produces a voltage signal. The airbag triggers when that voltage reaches a threshold.

T2-1 [2.7 points]. A spring-mounted capacitor

Two parallel plates of area \(A\) are separated by an air gap small compared with their lateral dimensions. One is fixed, and the other is attached to a wall by a spring of stiffness \(k\). The spring is unstressed at separation \(d\), where the capacitance is \(C_0=\epsilon_0 A/d\). Give the plates charges \(+Q\) and \(-Q\) and let the system equilibrate. Use vacuum permittivity \(\epsilon_0\) for air.

T2-1.1 [0.8 points]. Find the electrical force \(F_E\) between the plates.

T2-1.2 [0.6 points]. Find the equilibrium displacement \(x\) of the spring-mounted plate.

T2-1.3 [0.4 points]. Express the equilibrium potential difference \(V\) in terms of \(Q,A,d,k\) and \(\epsilon_0\).

T2-1.4 [0.3 points]. Find the ratio \(C/C_0\), where \(C=Q/V\), using the same parameters.

T2-1.5 [0.6 points]. Find the total stored energy \(U\) in terms of the given parameters.

T2-2 [2.1 points]. Grounded middle plate

In Fig. 2 the middle plate has negligible mass, carries a load of mass \(M\) and is attached to two springs, each of stiffness \(k\). The fixed outer plates are held at \(+V\) and \(-V\). All three plates are parallel with area \(A\), and the middle plate has negligible thickness. With no acceleration the two gaps are both \(d\), small compared with the plate dimensions. The connecting wire does not impede motion.

In parts 2 and 3 the vehicle acceleration is constant, oscillations are absent and the components are at rest relative to the vehicle. The middle plate is displaced by \(x\). In switch position \(\alpha\) it is grounded directly.

T2-2.1 [0.4 points]. Find the charge on each capacitor as a function of \(x\).

T2-2.2 [0.4 points]. Find the net electrical force \(F_E(x)\) on the middle plate.

T2-2.3 [0.2 points]. Simplify \(F_E\) for \(x\ll d\), keeping the leading term in the displacement.

T2-2.4 [0.7 points]. Write the combined spring and electric force as \(-k_{\rm eff}x\) and find \(k_{\rm eff}\).

T2-2.5 [0.4 points]. Express the constant vehicle acceleration \(a\) in terms of \(x\).

T2-3 [1.7 points]. Capacitor-coupled middle plate

In switch position \(\beta\) the middle plate is connected to ground through a capacitor of capacitance \(C_S\). The capacitors are initially uncharged.

T2-3.1 [1.5 points]. Find the voltage \(V_S(x)\) across \(C_S\) when the plate is displaced by \(x\).

T2-3.2 [0.2 points]. Simplify \(V_S\) for \(x\ll d\), dropping \(x^2\) compared with \(d^2\).

T2-4 [3.5 points]. Threshold and response time

Now consider the transient motion after braking begins abruptly. Mechanical equilibrium in the vehicle frame is no longer assumed. Neglect friction. Use \(d=1.0\) cm, \(A=2.5\times10^{-2}\) m², \(k=4.2\times10^3\) N/m, \(\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\) F/m, \(V=12\) V and \(M=0.15\) kg.

T2-4.1 [0.6 points]. Calculate the ratio of the electrical force in 2.3 to the total spring force in magnitude. Show that the electrical force is negligible.

Electric forces may likewise be neglected in switch position \(\beta\).

T2-4.2 [0.6 points]. A vehicle initially moving at constant velocity brakes suddenly with constant deceleration of magnitude \(a\). Find the maximum plate displacement symbolically.

Set the switch to \(\beta\). The trigger voltage is \(V_S=0.15\) V. The model should separate ordinary braking below \(g=9.8\) m/s² from braking that reaches the threshold.

T2-4.3 [0.6 points]. What value of \(C_S\) gives this threshold?

In the model collision the vehicle decelerates at \(g\) while the driver's head continues at its original speed.

T2-4.4 [0.8 points]. Estimate the driver's head-to-wheel distance, then find the time \(t_1\) for the head to reach the steering wheel.

T2-4.5 [0.9 points]. Find the trigger time \(t_2\) and compare it with \(t_1\). Does the airbag respond in time in this model, assuming instantaneous inflation once triggered?
