Problem T1 [10 points]. Gravity in a neutron interferometer

The Colella–Overhauser–Werner experiment probes the effect of gravity on neutron matter waves. Neutrons enter through IN. Ideal beam splitters (BS) and mirrors (M) send them along two paths around a diamond to outputs OUT1 and OUT2. A typical enclosed area is a few square centimetres and a typical de Broglie wavelength is \(10^{-10}\,\mathrm m\).

With the interferometer horizontal, all neutrons leave through OUT1. Tilting it through \(\phi\) about the incoming beam redistributes the intensity between the outputs. The plane is horizontal at \(\phi=0\) and vertical at \(\phi=90^\circ\), with the outputs above the tilt axis.

Geometry 2 pts

1.1 [1 pts]. Using the geometric parameters \(a,\theta,\phi\) shown, find the area \(A\) enclosed by the two paths.
1.2 [1 pts]. How far above the horizontal plane through the tilt axis is OUT1? Express the height \(H\) in terms of the geometric parameters.

Optical path length 6 pts

Here optical path length counts wavelengths. When the wavelength varies along a path, \(N_{\rm opt}=\int ds/\lambda\). The first-order expansion \((1+x)^\alpha\simeq1+\alpha x\) may be used for small corrections.

1.3 [3 pts]. Find the optical path difference \(\Delta N_{\rm opt}\) at tilt \(\phi\). Use \(a,\theta,\phi\), neutron mass \(M\), incident wavelength \(\lambda_0\), gravitational acceleration \(g\) and Planck’s constant \(h\).
1.4 [1 pts]. Introduce \(V=h^2/(gM^2)\). Express \(\Delta N_{\rm opt}\) using only \(A,V,\lambda_0,\phi\). Evaluate \(V\) for \(M=1.675\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}\), \(g=9.800\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\) and \(h=6.626\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}\).
1.5 [2 pts]. How many intensity cycles does OUT1 pass through as \(\phi\) increases from \(-90^\circ\) to \(90^\circ\)? One cycle is a change from high intensity to low and back to high.

Experimental data 2 pts

1.6 [1 pts]. An experiment with \(a=3.600\,\mathrm{cm}\) and \(\theta=22.10^\circ\) gives \(19.00\) complete cycles. Determine \(\lambda_0\).
1.7 [1 pts]. In another experiment of the same type, neutrons of wavelength \(\lambda_0=0.2000\,\mathrm{nm}\) give \(30.00\) cycles. Find the area \(A\).
