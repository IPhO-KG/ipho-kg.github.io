Problem T2 [10 points]. Watching a rod in motion

The pinhole of a camera is opposite the origin of the \(x\) axis at perpendicular distance \(D\). Its exposure is very short. Equally spaced marks along the axis let the observer infer apparent positions and lengths from a picture. The rod has rest length \(L\) and moves along positive \(x\) with constant velocity \(v\).

Apparent and actual positions 1.5 pts

A small piece of the rod appears at \(\tilde x\) on the picture. Its actual coordinate \(x\) is measured at exposure time in the camera frame. You may use \(\beta=v/c\), \(\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}\) and \(c=3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\).

2.1 [0.6 pts]. Express \(x\) in terms of the apparent coordinate and the given parameters.
2.2 [0.9 pts]. Find the inverse relation giving \(\tilde x\) for a specified actual coordinate \(x\).

Apparent length 3 pts

2.3 [1.5 pts]. The rod’s centre is actually at \(x_0\) when the exposure is made. What apparent length \(\tilde L(x_0)\) does the picture show?
2.4 [1.5 pts]. Choose how the apparent length changes with time: (a) it rises to a maximum, then falls; (b) it falls to a minimum, then rises; (c) it always decreases; (d) it always increases.

Symmetric picture 3 pts

On one photograph the apparent positions of the two ends are equally distant from the pinhole.

2.5 [0.8 pts]. Find the apparent length in this symmetric picture.
2.6 [1 pts]. What is the actual coordinate \(x_0\) of the midpoint at exposure time?
2.7 [1.2 pts]. Where does the rod’s midpoint appear? Give the distance \(\ell\) from its image to the image of the leading end.

Early and late pictures 2.5 pts

The first picture is taken when the rod is very far away and approaching, and the last when it is very far away and receding. One shows a length of \(1.00\,\mathrm m\) and the other \(3.00\,\mathrm m\).

2.8 [0.5 pts]. Assign the two lengths to the early and late pictures.
2.9 [1 pts]. Determine the rod’s velocity \(v\).
2.10 [0.6 pts]. What is the rest length \(L\)?
2.11 [0.4 pts]. Find the apparent length in the symmetric photograph.
