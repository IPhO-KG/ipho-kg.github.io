Problem T2 [10 points]. An electrified soap bubble

A spherical soap bubble of radius \(R_0\) contains air of density \(\rho_i\) at temperature \(T_i\). The ambient air has density \(\rho_a\), pressure \(P_a\) and temperature \(T_a\). The film has density \(\rho_s\), thickness \(t\ll R_0\) and surface tension \(\gamma\). The mass of soap and \(\gamma\) are independent of temperature. An increase \(dA\) in the area of one soap–air interface requires surface energy \(dE=\gamma\,dA\).

T2-2.1 [1.7 pts]. Express \(\rho_iT_i/(\rho_aT_a)\) in terms of \(\gamma,P_a,R_0\).
T2-2.2 [0.4 pts]. Evaluate \(\rho_iT_i/(\rho_aT_a)-1\) for \(\gamma=0.0250\,\mathrm{N/m}\), \(R_0=1.00\,\mathrm{cm}\) and \(P_a=1.013\times10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}\).
T2-2.3 [2 pts]. The air initially trapped in the bubble is warmer than its surroundings. Find the lowest \(T_i\) at which the bubble can float in still air. Use \(T_a=300\,\mathrm K\), \(\rho_s=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\), \(\rho_a=1.30\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\), \(t=100\,\mathrm{nm}\) and \(g=9.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\).

After a while the trapped air reaches thermal equilibrium with the surroundings. In still air the bubble now sinks.

T2-2.4 [1.6 pts]. Find the minimum updraught speed \(u\) needed to hold the bubble stationary. Use the low-speed Stokes drag \(F=6\pi\eta R_0u\), where \(\eta\) is the dynamic viscosity of air. Neglect the radius change on cooling. Give the leading term in terms of \(\rho_s,R_0,g,t,\eta\).
T2-2.5 [0.4 pts]. Evaluate \(u\) for \(\eta=1.8\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg/(m\,s)}\).

Neglect surface tension throughout the remaining parts. Starting from thermal equilibrium, give the initially neutral bubble a uniformly distributed charge \(q\).

T2-2.6 [2 pts]. Find the equation determining the new equilibrium radius \(R_1\) in terms of \(R_0,P_a,q\) and the permittivity of free space \(\varepsilon_0\).
T2-2.7 [0.7 pts]. If \(q^2/(\varepsilon_0R_0^4)\ll P_a\), the radius increase \(\Delta R=R_1-R_0\) is small. Obtain its leading term using \((1+x)^n\simeq1+nx\) for \(|x|\ll1\).
T2-2.8 [1.2 pts]. What charge magnitude gives neutral buoyancy in still air? Express it in terms of \(\varepsilon_0,t,R_0,\rho_s,\rho_a,P_a\) and calculate its value using \(\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}\).
