Problem T3 [10 points]. Scattering of an ion by a neutral atom

An ion of mass \(m\) and charge \(Q\) approaches a neutral atom of mass \(M\gg m\) from far away. The initial speed \(v_0\) is nonrelativistic and the impact parameter is \(b\). At each instant the electric field \(\mathbf E\) of the ion induces an atomic dipole moment \(\mathbf p=\alpha\mathbf E\), where \(\alpha\) is the electrical polarisability. Neglect radiation losses.

T3-3.1 [1.2 pts]. A dipole centred at \(O\) consists of charges \(\pm q\) separated by \(2a\), giving \(p=2aq\). Obtain its axial field vector \(\mathbf E_p\) at distance \(r\gg a\) from \(O\) in the direction of \(\mathbf p\).
T3-3.2 [3 pts]. Find the force \(\mathbf f\) exerted by the polarised atom on the ion. Show that either sign of \(Q\) gives an attraction.
T3-3.3 [0.9 pts]. Find the interaction potential energy at separation \(r\), taking its value at infinity to be zero.
T3-3.4 [2.4 pts]. Find the closest approach \(r_{\min}\) of the passing ion to the atom.
T3-3.5 [2.5 pts]. For \(b<b_0\) the ion spirals inward, becomes neutral and leaves the atom charged. Find the cross section \(A=\pi b_0^2\) for this charge-exchange process.
