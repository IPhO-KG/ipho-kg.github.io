Problem T2 [10 pts]. Absolute measurements of electrical quantities

How can the ohm and ampere be realised using mechanical measurements? Nineteenth-century experiments used rotating conductors and balances sensitive to the forces between currents.

1 3.5 pts Kelvin’s determination of the ohm

A closed circular coil of radius \(a\), with \(N\) turns and resistance \(R\), rotates at constant angular speed \(\omega\) about a vertical diameter in a uniform horizontal field \(\mathbf B_0=B_0\mathbf e_x\). Neglect self-inductance.

1 1.5 pts Find the induced emf \(\mathcal E\) and the mean power \(\langle P\rangle\) needed to maintain rotation. The period average is \(\langle X\rangle=T^{-1}\int_0^T X(t)\,dt\).

2 2 pts A small central magnetic needle turns slowly in the horizontal plane and cannot follow the rapidly rotating coil. In steady operation it settles at a small angle \(\theta\) to \(\mathbf B_0\). Obtain \(R\) from \(\theta\) and the apparatus parameters.

The following periodic averages and antiderivative may be used:

\[\langle\sin u\rangle=\langle\cos u\rangle=\langle\sin u\cos u\rangle=0,\qquad \langle\sin^2u\rangle=\langle\cos^2u\rangle=\tfrac12 .\]
\[\int x^n\,dx=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\qquad(n\ne-1).\]

2 2.5 pts Rayleigh and Sidgwick’s method

In Lorenz’s arrangement two identical metal discs D and D′ of radius \(b\) share a conducting shaft SS′, driven at adjustable angular speed \(\omega\). Each disc is surrounded by a fixed coil of radius \(a\) and \(N\) turns. The two coils carry current \(I\) in opposite directions.

3 2 pts Find the emf between rim contacts 1 and 4. The coil separation is much larger than \(a\), and \(a\gg b\). Take each disc’s field to be uniform and equal to that at the centre of its own coil.

4 0.5 pts Brushes at 1 and 4 join the discs to branch 1–2–3–4, containing galvanometer G. Adjust rotation until G reads zero. Determine the resistance \(R\).

3 4 pts The current balance

Six identical single-turn rings of radius \(a\) are in series. Rings \(C_1,C_3,C_4,C_6\) are fixed in two horizontal planes a short distance \(2h\) apart. The moving rings \(C_2,C_5\) hang from balance arms of length \(d\). At equilibrium they lie halfway between the planes. The current \(I\) produces an upward force on \(C_2\) and a downward force on \(C_5\). A mass \(m\) at distance \(x\) from pivot O restores equilibrium.

5 1 pt Find the force \(F\) exerted by \(C_1\) on \(C_2\). Approximate the force per unit length by that for two infinite parallel straight wires.

6 1 pt Use equilibrium to find \(I\) in terms of the apparatus parameters. Neglect interactions between the left and right groups of rings.

7 2 pts The balance itself, excluding \(m\) and the suspended parts, has mass \(M\) and centre of mass G a distance \(l\) below O. For a small tilt \(C_2\) rises by \(\delta z\) while \(C_5\) drops by the same amount. Find the limiting displacement below which the balance moves back towards equilibrium when released. Treat the ring centres as approximately aligned.

For small \(u\), use \((1\pm u)^{-1}\simeq1\mp u+u^2\) or \((1\pm u^2)^{-1}\simeq1\mp u^2\). For small angles \(\sin\theta\simeq\tan\theta\).
