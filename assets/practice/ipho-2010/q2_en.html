Problem T2 [10 pts]. Chimney physics

A furnace generates smoke at a volume rate \(B\) and exhausts it through a vertical chimney of height \(h\) and cross section \(A\). The furnace gas is at temperature \(T_{\mathrm{s}}\) and the outside air at \(T_{\mathrm{a}}\). The smoke speed inside the furnace is negligible. Smoke and air have the same density at equal temperatures and pressures. Treat the gas in the furnace as ideal.

The outside pressure is hydrostatic. Neglect density changes with height, both in the outside air and for the smoke throughout the flue. Along the gas stream use Bernoulli’s equation. Take \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\).

\[\tfrac12\rho v^2+\rho gz+p=\mathrm{const}.\]

1 5.1 pts Draught and chimney height

1(a) 3.5 pts Find the least height that lets all the smoke escape. Express it through \(B,A,T_{\mathrm{a}},g\) and \(\Delta T=T_{\mathrm{s}}-T_{\mathrm{a}}\). Use this minimum height in all subsequent parts.

1(b) 0.5 pts Two chimneys with equal cross sections serve identical furnaces at \(400\,^\circ\mathrm C\). A height of \(100\,\mathrm m\) is required at an ambient temperature of \(-30\,^\circ\mathrm C\). Find the required height at \(30\,^\circ\mathrm C\).

1(c) 0.6 pts Sketch the gas speed against height for a chimney of constant cross section. Mark the inlet.

1(d) 0.5 pts How does the smoke pressure vary with height?

2 2.4 pts Solar chimney power plant

Sunlight heats the air below a collector of area \(S\), open around its rim. The warmed air rises up a chimney and drives a turbine, while fresh air is drawn in. The incident solar power per horizontal collector area is \(G\). Assume it all heats the air, with constant specific heat \(c\).

2(a) 2 pts Find the efficiency, defined as the ratio between the kinetic energy of the air stream and the solar energy used to heat that air before it enters the chimney.

2(b) 0.4 pts Sketch how efficiency depends on chimney height.

3 1 pt The Manzanares prototype

The Spanish prototype has a \(195\,\mathrm m\) chimney of radius \(5\,\mathrm m\) and a circular collector \(244\,\mathrm m\) across. Use \(c=1012\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}\), hot-air density \(\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=0.9\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\) and \(T_{\mathrm{a}}=295\,\mathrm K\). A representative solar irradiance on a sunny day is \(G=150\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\).

3(a) 0.3 pts Calculate the efficiency of the prototype.

3(b) 0.4 pts What power can the prototype deliver under these assumptions?

3(c) 0.3 pts Estimate the energy produced on a typical sunny day, stating the duration of sunshine assumed.

4 1.5 pts Air temperature rise and mass flow

4(a) 1 pt Derive the air temperature increase between the surroundings and the chimney inlet and evaluate it for the prototype.

4(b) 0.5 pts Find the mass of air passing through the plant per second.
