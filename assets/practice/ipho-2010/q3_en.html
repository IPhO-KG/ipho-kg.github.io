Problem T3 [10 pts]. A simple model of the atomic nucleus

In parts 1–4 the nucleus contains \(A\gg1\) nucleons, with nearly equal proton and neutron numbers, \(Z\simeq N\simeq A/2\). The attractive nuclear interaction is short-ranged and binds neighbouring nucleons.

1 1.3 pts Nucleon packing

Model the nucleus as a sphere of volume \(V\) filled with hard touching spheres of radius \(r_N=0.85\,\mathrm{fm}\), with \(1\,\mathrm{fm}=10^{-15}\,\mathrm m\). Their material occupies volume \(AV_N<V\). The packing fraction is \(f=AV_N/V\), where \(V_N=4\pi r_N^3/3\).

1(a) 0.3 pts Determine \(f\) for an infinite simple cubic lattice with nucleons at its sites. Use that value from here on. If you did not obtain it, take \(f=1/2\).

1(b) 1 pt Estimate the average mass density \(\rho_m\), charge density \(\rho_c\) and radius \(R\) for \(A\) nucleons. Use the mean nucleon mass \(m_N=1.67\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}\) and elementary charge \(e=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C\).

2 1.9 pts Volume and surface binding energies

2 1.9 pts The binding energy \(E_b\) is the work required to separate the nucleus into individual nucleons. A bulk nucleon contributes \(a_V=15.8\,\mathrm{MeV}\), while a surface nucleon contributes approximately \(a_V/2\). Obtain \(E_b\) in terms of \(A,a_V,f\), taking the outer layer into account. Use \(1\,\mathrm{MeV}=1.602\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm J\).

3 0.7 pts Coulomb correction

For a uniformly charged sphere of radius \(R\) and total charge \(Q_0\), the electrostatic energy is given below. Take \(\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}\).

\[U_c=\frac{3Q_0^2}{20\pi\varepsilon_0 R}.\]

3(a) 0.4 pts Apply the sphere formula to a nucleus. Remove proton self-interaction by replacing \(Z^2\) with \(Z(Z-1)\) and retain this correction in the remaining parts.

3(b) 0.3 pts Write the complete binding energy with the volume, surface and Coulomb terms.

4 2.3 pts Division into equal fragments

An initially stationary nucleus with \(A\) nucleons splits into two equal spherical fragments. Each has radius \(R(A/2)\).

4(a) 1.3 pts Find the total fragment kinetic energy \(E_k\) when their centres are \(d\ge2R(A/2)\) apart.

4(b) 1 pt Evaluate \(E_k\) in MeV at \(d=2R(A/2)\) for \(A=100,150,200,250\). Estimate the mass numbers for which division is energetically possible in this model.

5 3.8 pts Cluster transfer reaction

An oxygen nucleus of kinetic energy \(50\,\mathrm{MeV}\) strikes a stationary iron target. An alpha cluster is transferred to the target, leaving nickel in an excited state. In the most probable reaction the outgoing carbon has the same velocity vector as the incident oxygen.

\[{}^{16}\mathrm O+{}^{54}\mathrm{Fe}\longrightarrow{}^{12}\mathrm C+{}^{58}\mathrm{Ni}^{*}.\]

The following ground-state masses are in atomic mass units. Use \(1\,\mathrm u=1.6605\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}\) and \(c=3.0\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\). Excitation energy \(E_*\) adds \(E_*/c^2\) to a nucleus’s mass.

\[M({}^{16}\mathrm O)=15.99491\,\mathrm u,\qquad M({}^{54}\mathrm{Fe})=53.93962\,\mathrm u .\]
\[M({}^{12}\mathrm C)=12.00000\,\mathrm u,\qquad M({}^{58}\mathrm{Ni})=57.93535\,\mathrm u .\]

5(a) 2.2 pts Find the excitation energy of \(^{58}\mathrm{Ni}\) in MeV.

5(b) 1.6 pts Nickel emits a gamma ray in the forward direction as it returns to the ground state. Find the nuclear recoil energy and the photon energy in the initial nickel rest frame. What photon energy is measured by a lab detector located ahead of the moving nucleus?
