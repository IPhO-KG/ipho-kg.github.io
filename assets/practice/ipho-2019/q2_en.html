Question T2 [10 pts]. The Physics of a Microwave Oven

T2-A [6.6 pts]. The magnetron

Eight resonators surround a cylindrical cathode of radius \(a\). The inner radius of the anode is \(b\). The evacuated gap contains a static electric field pointing from anode to cathode. A uniform magnetic field \(\mathbf B=B_0\hat{\mathbf z}\) points out of the page.

Neighbouring resonators have opposite charges at the operating frequency \(f=2.45\,\mathrm{GHz}\). Blue lines show the alternating field only. In steady operation its amplitude is about one third of the static field. Electrons transfer about four fifths of the energy gained from the supply to this alternating field.

T2-A1 [0.4 pts]. Estimate the natural frequency using Fig. 1(b). The slot acts as a capacitor and the cylindrical surface carries a uniform sheet current. Its field is 0.6 times that of an infinite solenoid with the same current per unit length. Use \(\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}\) and \(\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}\). Use the stated operating frequency in later parts.
T2-A2 [1.5 pts]. A free electron of charge \(-e\) and mass \(m\) moves in uniform fields \(\mathbf E=-E_0\hat{\mathbf y}\) and \(\mathbf B=B_0\hat{\mathbf z}\). For initial velocities \(+3(E_0/B_0)\hat{\mathbf x}\) and \(-3(E_0/B_0)\hat{\mathbf x}\) find the mean velocity and draw each laboratory trajectory for \(0<t<4\pi m/(eB_0)\).
T2-A3 [0.4 pts]. In the magnetron the gap is \(15\,\mathrm{mm}\), \(B_0=0.3\,\mathrm T\) and the electron energy never exceeds \(800\,\mathrm{eV}\). Estimate an upper bound for the orbit radius in the approximately inertial drift frame. Use \(m=9.1\cdot10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}\) and \(e=1.6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C\).

T2-A5 [1.2 pts]. For each pair AB, AC, BC, DE, DF and EF in Fig. 3 determine whether drift increases or decreases their angular separation. All six electrons are at equal radius.
T2-A6 [0.8 pts]. Figure 4 shows one focused spoke S. Draw all remaining spokes, indicate the rotation direction and calculate their mean angular speed \(\omega_s\).

T2-B [3.4 pts]. Microwave absorption in water

T2-B1 [0.5 pts]. A dipole of fixed magnitude \(p_0=qd\) makes an angle \(\theta(t)\) with the \(x\) axis. The applied field is \(\mathbf E=E(t)\hat{\mathbf x}\). Find the torque magnitude and the instantaneous power \(H_i\) delivered to the dipole.
T2-B2 [0.5 pts]. The polarization lags behind the field. For \(E_x=E_0\sin\omega t\) it is \(P_x=\beta\varepsilon_0 E_0\sin(\omega t-\delta)\), with \(\omega=2\pi f\). Find the cycle-averaged absorbed power per unit volume.
T2-B3 [1.1 pts]. A wave propagates into water along \(z\). The index is \(n=\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}\). Electric energy density is \(\varepsilon_r\varepsilon_0 E^2/2\), with equal mean electric and magnetic contributions. Express the mean flux \(I(z)\) in terms of its surface value.
