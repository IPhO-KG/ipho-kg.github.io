Problem T2 [10 points]. Nonlinear electrical circuits

T2-A [3 pts]. Stationary states and stability

The semiconductor switch \(X\) has the piecewise linear S-shaped characteristic shown below. In the interior of the interval bounded by the holding voltage \(U_{\rm h}=4.00\,\mathrm V\) and the threshold voltage \(U_{\rm th}=10.0\,\mathrm V\), three current values correspond to a single voltage. The extrapolated upper branch goes through the origin.

T2-A1 [0.4 pts]. Read the upper and lower branch resistances \(R_{\rm on},R_{\rm off}\) from the graph. Determine \(I_0,R_{\rm int}\) for the middle branch, \(I=I_0-U/R_{\rm int}\).

Place \(X\) in series with a resistor \(R\), inductor \(L\) and ideal source of emf \(\mathcal E\). A stationary state has time-independent current.

T2-A2 [1 pts]. For a given emf, how many stationary states are possible with \(R=3.00\,\Omega\)? List the possible numbers for \(R=1.00\,\Omega\) as well.
T2-A3 [0.6 pts]. For \(R=3.00\,\Omega\), \(L=1.00\,\mu\mathrm H\) and \(\mathcal E=15.0\,\mathrm V\), find the stationary current and the voltage across \(X\).
T2-A4 [1 pts]. Trace the response to a small positive or negative current perturbation about A3. Is this stationary state stable?

T2-B [5 pts]. A relaxation oscillator and an antenna

Connect a capacitor \(C=1.00\,\mu\mathrm F\) in parallel with \(X\). Feed this parallel combination through \(R=3.00\,\Omega\) from \(\mathcal E=15.0\,\mathrm V\). Between switches the operating point moves along one of the outer branches.

T2-B1 [1.8 pts]. Sketch the closed cycle in the \((U,I_X)\) plane and indicate its orientation. Explain the continuous parts and jumps with equations and a diagram.
T2-B2 [1.9 pts]. Find expressions and numerical values for the times spent on the two branches, and for the period \(T\). Treat switching as instantaneous.
T2-B3 [0.7 pts]. Estimate the cycle-averaged power dissipated by \(X\) to order of magnitude.

The switch drives one end of a straight wire antenna of length \(s\). Its other end is free. Standing electromagnetic waves travel along the wire at speed \(c\), driven at the oscillator fundamental \(1/T\).

T2-B4 [0.6 pts]. What resonant antenna length should be used if \(s\le1\,\mathrm{km}\)?

T2-C [2 pts]. The neuristor

An excitable system responds to a sufficiently strong stimulus with a single complete excursion before returning to rest. Its electronic analogue is called a neuristor. Set the source in circuit B to \(\mathcal E^{\prime}=12.0\,\mathrm V\) and wait for equilibrium. At \(t_0\) switch to \(15.0\,\mathrm V\), then return abruptly to \(\mathcal E^{\prime}\) after \(\tau<T\).

T2-C1 [1.2 pts]. Sketch the current through \(X\) versus time for both \(\tau<\tau_{\rm crit}\) and \(\tau>\tau_{\rm crit}\).
T2-C2 [0.6 pts]. Derive and evaluate the critical pulse duration \(\tau_{\rm crit}\).
T2-C3 [0.2 pts]. For \(\tau=1.00\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm s\), does the circuit act as a neuristor?
