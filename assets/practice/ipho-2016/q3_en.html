T3-A [6 pts]. Particle acceleration

T3-A2 [0.8 pts]. A future electron–proton machine would use \(60.0\,\mathrm{GeV}\) electrons. Obtain the leading nonzero approximation for \(\Delta=(c-v)/c\) in terms of \(V\) and constants, and evaluate it for these electrons.
T3-A3 [1 pts]. An ideal ring of circumference \(L\) bends a noninteracting proton beam with a uniform magnetic field. Obtain \(B\) in terms of the proton energy \(E\), \(L\) and constants, and evaluate it for \(E=7.00\,\mathrm{TeV}\). Approximations should preserve the precision of the data.
T3-A4 [1 pts]. For nonrelativistic uniform circular motion the radiated power depends only on acceleration \(a\), charge \(q\), light speed \(c\) and vacuum permittivity \(\varepsilon_0\). Infer the dependence by dimensional analysis.

The numerical coefficient in A4 is \(1/(6\pi)\). For relativistic circular motion there is a further factor \(\gamma^4\), where \(\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}\).
